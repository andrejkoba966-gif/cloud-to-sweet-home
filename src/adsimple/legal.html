Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

AD Simple ("we", "us", "our") provides performance marketing services — content production, Google Ads, Meta Ads and AI-powered creative — to businesses in the Netherlands and beyond. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who we are

AD Simple is operated by Illia Zalivan, based in Zeeland, Netherlands. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us using the details in Section 8.

2. What data we collect

We only collect data that you choose to give us:

  • Strategy session form: company name, country code and phone number, submitted when you request a free strategy session.
  • Direct contact: your name, email address and any details you share when you email or call us directly.
  • Approximate location: when you open the strategy session form, we use your IP address to guess your country so the phone country code is pre-filled. We do not store your IP address or exact location.

3. How we use your data

We use the data you submit to:

  • Contact you about the free strategy session you requested;
  • Respond to questions sent by email or phone;
  • Prepare and deliver our marketing services if we start working together.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising to you or anyone else.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your data based on your consent (submitting the form or contacting us) and, where we start a client relationship, to perform that contract.

5. Who we share data with

Strategy session requests are delivered to our team via a secure messaging integration (Telegram), so that submitted company name, country code and phone number pass through that provider to reach us. We do not share your data with any other third party, and we never sell it.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep strategy session requests and correspondence for as long as needed to respond to you and, if you become a client, for the duration of our working relationship plus any period required by Dutch tax and accounting law. You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time — see Section 7.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • Ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data;
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.
